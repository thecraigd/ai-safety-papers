Hosting and delivery

The website's files are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and delivered through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which acts as the content delivery network and security layer for theguardrail.net. The paper data that the pages load from data.theguardrail.net is stored in Cloudflare R2 and also delivered by Cloudflare. When you visit the site, Cloudflare and GitHub process connection data such as your IP address, the date and time of access, the page or file requested and your browser's user agent, in order to deliver the site and protect it against attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Data may be transferred to the United States. Both providers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses for such transfers.

Fonts, scripts and stylesheets, including the cookie banner, are served from theguardrail.net itself. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other font or script provider when you visit; Google Analytics is only loaded if you consent, and your profile picture only if you sign in (see below).

Cloudflare Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement

User accounts and saved papers

Reading the site never requires an account. If you choose to sign in to save papers, you do so with Google, GitHub or Microsoft (OAuth). We then receive your email address, your name, a link to your profile picture and your account ID at that provider. We store these, together with the papers you save and the date your account was created, in a database run by Cloudflare (Cloudflare D1), accessed through our account service at api.theguardrail.net (Cloudflare Workers).

Signing in sets two cookies on api.theguardrail.net: session, which keeps you signed in for 30 days or until you sign out, and oauth_state, which protects the sign-in against forgery and expires after 10 minutes. Both are strictly necessary for the sign-in you ask for.

While you are signed in, your profile picture is loaded directly from your sign-in provider's servers (for example Google or GitHub), which receive your IP address when it loads. The account service may write short diagnostic messages, which can include your email address, to Cloudflare's logs to operate and fix the service; Cloudflare keeps these for a limited time.

The legal basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing the saved-papers service you requested. You can export your data or delete your account at any time in account settings; deleting the account deletes your saved papers with it.

Storage on your device

The site stores a few small items in your browser to provide functions you choose. None of them is used for tracking:

  • cc_cookie (cookie): your cookie choices, so the banner does not reappear.
  • theme (local storage): your colour theme, if you choose light or dark instead of following your system.
  • shortcuts (local storage): set only if you turn keyboard shortcuts off.
  • pendingSavePaperId (session storage): the paper you asked to save while signing in, removed once it is saved.
  • guardrail_bookmarks (local storage): papers saved on this device with an earlier version of the site. They are moved to your account, and removed from the browser, when you sign in.

These are strictly necessary for functions you request (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete them at any time in your browser's settings.

Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to understand how the site is used and to improve it. Google Analytics is only loaded, from googletagmanager.com, after you actively consent to analytics cookies in our cookie banner. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG).

When enabled, Google Analytics processes information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser information, and interactions. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the United States, based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. We set the GA4 data retention period to 14 months.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and consent

We use only the cookies described above: the consent cookie, the sign-in cookies if you sign in, and, only with your consent, Google Analytics cookies (such as _ga and _ga_<id>, with lifetimes of up to two years). We do not use marketing or advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to The Weekly Ten by email, the sign-up form sends your email address directly to Buttondown (Buttondown, LLC), which manages the list and sends the emails, and opens a Buttondown page in a new window. We send one email per week with that week's list of papers, ranked by language models. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Buttondown Privacy Policy

Links to other sites

The site links to other services, including arXiv (papers and PDFs), Ko-fi (donations), GitHub, BlueDot Impact and, for sharing a week's list, X, LinkedIn and Bluesky. These are ordinary links: nothing is sent to these services until you follow one, after which their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the GDPR, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. To exercise your rights, contact us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Benutzerkonten und gespeicherte Paper

Zum Lesen der Website ist nie ein Konto erforderlich. Wenn Sie sich anmelden, um Paper zu speichern, geschieht das über Google, GitHub oder Microsoft (OAuth). Wir erhalten dann Ihre E-Mail-Adresse, Ihren Namen, einen Link zu Ihrem Profilbild und Ihre Konto-ID bei diesem Anbieter. Wir speichern diese Angaben zusammen mit den von Ihnen gespeicherten Papern und dem Erstellungsdatum Ihres Kontos in einer von Cloudflare betriebenen Datenbank (Cloudflare D1), auf die unser Kontodienst unter api.theguardrail.net (Cloudflare Workers) zugreift.

Bei der Anmeldung werden zwei Cookies auf api.theguardrail.net gesetzt: session, das Sie 30 Tage lang oder bis zur Abmeldung angemeldet hält, und oauth_state, das die Anmeldung vor Fälschung schützt und nach 10 Minuten abläuft. Beide sind für die von Ihnen gewünschte Anmeldung unbedingt erforderlich.

Solange Sie angemeldet sind, wird Ihr Profilbild direkt von den Servern Ihres Anmeldeanbieters (z. B. Google oder GitHub) geladen, die dabei Ihre IP-Adresse erhalten. Der Kontodienst kann kurze Diagnosemeldungen, die Ihre E-Mail-Adresse enthalten können, in die Protokolle von Cloudflare schreiben, um den Dienst zu betreiben und Fehler zu beheben; Cloudflare speichert diese für begrenzte Zeit.

Rechtsgrundlage ist die Vertragserfüllung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO): die Bereitstellung des von Ihnen angeforderten Dienstes zum Speichern von Papern. Sie können Ihre Daten jederzeit exportieren oder Ihr Konto in den Kontoeinstellungen löschen; mit dem Konto werden auch Ihre gespeicherten Paper gelöscht.

Speicherung auf Ihrem Gerät

Die Website speichert einige kleine Einträge in Ihrem Browser, um von Ihnen gewählte Funktionen bereitzustellen. Keiner davon dient dem Tracking:

  • cc_cookie (Cookie): Ihre Cookie-Auswahl, damit das Banner nicht erneut erscheint.
  • theme (Local Storage): Ihr Farbschema, wenn Sie hell oder dunkel wählen, statt der Systemeinstellung zu folgen.
  • shortcuts (Local Storage): nur gesetzt, wenn Sie die Tastaturkürzel ausschalten.
  • pendingSavePaperId (Session Storage): das Paper, das Sie während der Anmeldung speichern wollten; wird nach dem Speichern entfernt.
  • guardrail_bookmarks (Local Storage): Paper, die mit einer früheren Version der Website auf diesem Gerät gespeichert wurden. Sie werden bei der Anmeldung in Ihr Konto übernommen und aus dem Browser entfernt.

Diese Einträge sind für von Ihnen gewünschte Funktionen unbedingt erforderlich (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG). Sie können sie jederzeit in den Einstellungen Ihres Browsers löschen.
