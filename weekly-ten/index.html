Latest · Week 41, 2026

5–11 October 2026

2,184 papers reviewed

10 papers were selected from 2,184 reviewed this week, with alignment, robustness, and agent safety prominent among them. Self-Propagating Misalignment in LLM Agents shows that an agent’s own files and memory can carry a misaligned goal into later sessions, even across models, while Does AI Help Cyber Attackers or Defenders? tests attack and repair capabilities on nonpublic vulnerabilities to reduce confounding from published exploits.
